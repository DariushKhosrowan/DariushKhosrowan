### About me

I'm **Dariush Khosrowan**, a developer with an academic background in **Electrical Engineering (Control)** from **Iran University of Science and Technology**. My work spans artificial intelligence, computer vision, data-driven modeling, and the software that connects algorithms to real-world applications.

I enjoy building from fundamentals: implementing neural networks, designing search algorithms, training vision models, and experimenting with reinforcement learning on physical robots. I'm especially interested in **machine learning, perception, and autonomous decision-making**.
